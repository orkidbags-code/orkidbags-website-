PRITAM & CO. — TRONICA CITY

Crafting bags that carry purpose, performance and everyday style.

Pritam & Co. is a bag manufacturing enterprise based in Tronica City, Loni, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 2019, its listed portfolio serves requirements across school, travel, office, lifestyle, promotional and utility categories.

The supplied company profile identifies the business as an individual / sole proprietorship and lists Mr Jatin Kukreja as proprietor. The ORKID logo is presented here as supplied by the website owner.
